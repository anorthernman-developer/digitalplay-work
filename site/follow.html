How the posts get made

We schedule posts with Buffer, a tool for queuing social posts. A person writes and checks every one. Nothing is posted automatically.

  1. A tool goes live

    A new tool is merged into one of the section repos and appears on the tools page.

  2. A draft gets written

    A short, plain post is drafted in the hub repo, in docs/launch-posts.md, so anyone can see what’s coming.

  3. Craig reads it and queues it

    Craig edits the draft and queues it in Buffer himself, for X and as a Substack Note.

  4. It goes out, and that’s it

    No bots replying, no tracking links. If you want to talk about a tool, come to Discussions.

About your data

X, Substack and Buffer are separate services with their own privacy policies. This site sets no cookies and doesn’t embed their widgets, so nothing loads from them until you click through.
