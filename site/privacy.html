Privacy

We don’t want your data.

Short version: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts. The longer version is below.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels.
  • No forms. We don’t collect names, email addresses or anything else.
  • Fonts, scripts and styles are all served from this site, so visiting doesn’t send anything to Google or anyone else.
  • The site is hosted on Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify handles technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver pages and keep the service secure. We don’t use it to identify or profile anyone.

The tools

  • The tools run in your browser. Your sounds, photos and words stay on your device and aren’t uploaded anywhere.
  • Presets you save are kept in your browser’s local storage. You can export them as a file, and clearing your browser’s site data removes them.
  • The starter tools in Art, Design, Coding and Writing make no network requests at all once they’ve loaded.

AI and your own Gemini key

A few music tools have an optional AI feature. If you choose to use it, you paste in your own Google Gemini API key.

  • Your key is stored only in your own browser’s local storage on that device. It isn’t sent to us, and we never see it.
  • When you press an AI button, the tool sends your request straight from your browser to Google’s Gemini API using your key. Google’s terms and privacy policy apply to that request.
  • Every tool works without AI. Leave the key box empty and nothing is ever sent.
  • To remove a key, use the tool’s clear button if it has one, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • Don’t paste a key on a shared or public computer.

Other sites we link to

We link to GitHub, X, Substack and Amazon. They’re separate services with their own policies, and nothing from them loads on this site until you follow a link.

Questions

Ask in GitHub Discussions, but please don’t post personal details there because it’s public.
